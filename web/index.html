DEPENDENCY DELIVERY POLICY

Dependencies live inside the Physical Lab experience.

Ordinary scientific Python packages are normal managed dependencies and remain available in both Safe and Full modes. The special dependency workflow is reserved for fragile native engines such as RADIA that can fail because of ABI, architecture, SDK or compiler issues. Physical Lab either reuses them, builds them, or keeps Safe mode available.

Verification & assumptions

The model distinguishes the angular bifurcation n_c from the volume-dependent threshold n_g(V). Numerical convergence does not establish physical validity, and agreement with measurements is validation evidence rather than proof of model truth.

The hysteresis view is a reduced-order apparatus-level description. H and τ must not be relabeled as contact-angle hysteresis, viscosity, pinning energy, or a hardware safety limit without an independently validated physical model.
